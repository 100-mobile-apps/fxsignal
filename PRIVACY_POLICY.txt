PRIVACY POLICY - FOREX SIGNALS LIVE
Effective date: September 29, 2026

Forex Signals Live ("the App") is operated by Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd
("we", "us", "our"). This policy describes how we handle information when
you use the App and its related online service. Contact us about this policy
at swalafblog@gmail.com.

1. INFORMATION WE HANDLE

Account information: If you register for the live service, we receive the
name, email address and password you provide. We store a password hash, not
your plaintext password. We also store your account creation and last-login
times, email verification status, selected markets/watchlist, and email and
push notification preferences. One-time verification and password-reset
codes are stored as hashes and stop being valid after 15 minutes. Their
database records are not automatically erased at expiry; they are removed
when the account is deleted.

Security and service information: The server uses your IP address and, for
some requests, your email address in temporary rate-limit records to protect
registration and authentication. Our web host may keep ordinary server
access and error logs, such as IP address, request time, requested route and
technical error details. The App requests market prices, signals and reports
from our API, which receives network information needed to respond.

Messages: After you verify your address, new-signal, result, weekly and
monthly report emails are enabled by default; daily report emails are off
by default. Our server may hold your address and the subject and content of
queued messages. If you contact support, we receive the information you
choose to send. If you tap an external contact link, the destination service
may receive your request and any message you choose to send.

Device and local information: The App stores an authentication token in
secure device storage. It keeps an account-profile cache (including name,
email and preferences), onboarding state and limited review/exit-prompt usage
data in local app storage. Calculator inputs are used for on-device
calculations; the App does not connect to your broker account or collect
payment-card or trading-account credentials through these features.

Optional push data: If Firebase push is configured, the App initializes
Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) at startup. FCM may process an app-instance
or device token. After sign-in, the App synchronizes signal, result and
report topic subscriptions with your preferences even if notification
display permission is denied; device settings control whether notifications
appear. Our server sends to topics and does not store device tokens. Push
may be unavailable if Firebase is not configured.

Demo mode: If the App was built without a live API URL, it uses simulated
local prices and results instead of creating an account on our server. A
demo profile containing the name, email and preferences you enter is saved
on your device. It remains after logout and is removed by Delete account.
App features that open external links or use platform/third-party services
can still involve those services as described below.

2. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We use this information to create and secure accounts, authenticate sign-in,
send verification and password-reset emails, remember preferences, provide
market and signal information, deliver the alerts and reports you select,
respond to support requests, prevent abuse, and maintain and troubleshoot the
service. We do not sell your account information or use this App to process
payments. Market data and modeled signals are general information, not a
record of your personal trades.

Where applicable, we process information to provide the service you request,
for security and service operations, with your choice to receive optional
notifications, and as required by law. You may turn off optional alerts and
emails as explained below.

3. SERVICE PROVIDERS AND EXTERNAL LINKS

Our hosting provider processes the account database and ordinary server
logs. Our email host or SMTP provider processes the address and content
necessary to deliver verification codes, alerts and reports. If push is
enabled, Google Firebase processes push-delivery information. The backend
requests price candles for selected markets from Twelve Data; it does not
need to send your account name or email to Twelve Data for that request.

The App uses Google Fonts, which may download font files and receive standard
network information such as your IP address. The App may ask Google Play to
show an in-app review prompt after certain usage; Google decides whether to
display it. If you choose to open WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Buy Me a Coffee, email
or other external links, those third parties handle information under their
own privacy terms. Opening an external link is your choice. We are not
responsible for the privacy practices of independent sites or services.

Depending on where you and our service providers are located, information
may be processed outside your country. We do not currently include an
advertising or third-party analytics SDK in the App.

4. YOUR CHOICES

You can change market, email and push preferences under Profile >
Notifications. Verified email recipients can also use the unsubscribe link
in an alert or report email. An email already queued before a preference
change or unsubscribe request may still be delivered. Your device settings
can block push messages. Disabling notifications does not stop essential
account messages such as verification and password-reset emails that you
request.

You can change your displayed name and password in the App. To request
access to, correction of, or deletion of other personal information, email
swalafblog@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a
request, and some information may be retained where required by law or for
security. Available rights vary by location.

5. ACCOUNT DELETION AND RETENTION

You can request account deletion in Profile > Delete account; the App asks
for your password. This removes your user record and associated verification
or reset-code records from the live database and clears the local session
token and cached profile when the App completes logout. Shared market
signals and aggregate results are not linked to your account and remain.

Important limitation: the current server does not automatically delete
previously queued, sent or failed email records when an account is deleted.
These records can contain your address and message content, and a pending
message could still be sent after deletion. Sent email queue records older
than 30 days are removed by an occasional worker cleanup; pending and failed
records have no automatic deletion time. Rate-limit records older than one
day are also cleared opportunistically. Hosting logs, backups and copies
already delivered to an email provider or inbox may be retained under those
providers' policies. For manual removal of remaining email records, contact
swalafblog@gmail.com. We will review the request and tell you what can be
removed. We do not promise immediate deletion of all copies.

6. SECURITY

The live App is intended to use HTTPS for API requests. We hash passwords,
use signed authentication tokens, keep the App token in secure device storage,
and use server-side access controls and rate limits. No internet or storage
system can be guaranteed completely secure. Keep your password private and
contact us if you suspect unauthorized account access.

7. CHILDREN

The App is intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly seek
personal information from children. If you believe a child has registered,
contact swalafblog@gmail.com so we can investigate and remove the account
subject to the retention limits above.
