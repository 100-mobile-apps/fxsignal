Forex Signals Live

Request account deletion

This page explains how to delete your Forex Signals Live account and request removal of related data. The service is operated by Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd. You can make a request even if you cannot sign in to the app.

Delete from the app

  1. Sign in to Forex Signals Live.
  2. Open Profile > Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your account password.

This removes your user profile and verification/reset-code records from the live database and signs you out on that device. Shared market signals and reports are not linked to your account.

Request deletion without signing in

Email us from the address registered to your account and ask for account and associated data deletion. If you cannot use that address, tell us so we can verify ownership another way. Do not send your password or verification code.

Email a deletion request

If the button does not open your email app, write to swalafblog@gmail.com with the subject "Forex Signals Live account deletion request". We will review and respond to your request after verifying account ownership.

What may remain

The current automatic in-app deletion does not remove pending, sent or failed email-queue records, which can contain your email address and message content; a pending email may still be delivered. Ask us to review and remove remaining email records as part of your request. Sent queue entries older than 30 days are removed during occasional worker cleanup; pending and failed entries do not have an automatic deletion deadline. Hosting logs, backups and email-provider copies may remain subject to their retention practices or legal requirements. We will explain what can be removed when we respond.

Privacy contact: Swiftmynd Technologies Ltd, swalafblog@gmail.com. Never include a password in an email request.
